## 📌 Project Overview

Supply chain management involves coordinating suppliers, inventory, production, shipments, and customer demand. Monitoring these interconnected operations is essential for identifying inefficiencies, controlling costs, and improving overall business performance.

This project uses **Microsoft Power BI** to develop a centralized analytics dashboard for a Samsung-themed supply chain and logistics scenario. It provides visibility into key operational and financial metrics, enabling stakeholders to monitor performance and identify areas requiring attention.

The dashboard is organized into five sections:

- **Overview:** Consolidated business performance and operational KPIs.
- **Supplier Information:** Supplier performance, procurement costs, lead times, and quality scores.
- **Inventory & Production:** Inventory valuation, stock levels, defective units, safety stock, and reorder points.
- **Shipments:** Shipment performance, delivery status, carrier delays, and logistics costs.
- **Customer:** Revenue distribution, profitability, sales channels, product performance, and discounts.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Monitor overall supply chain and financial performance through centralized KPIs.
- Evaluate supplier efficiency using lead times, procurement costs, and quality scores.
- Analyze inventory levels and identify potential stock management issues.
- Monitor defective products and compare current stock against safety stock and reorder points.
- Assess shipment performance across logistics providers and delivery statuses.
- Identify revenue and profit contributions across sales channels and product categories.
- Track monthly revenue, profitability, and growth trends to support business analysis.

## 📈 Key Business Takeaways

The dashboard enables several important business observations:

1. **Supplier Performance:** Supplier lead times and quality scores can be compared to identify suppliers that may require further evaluation.

2. **Inventory Management:** Comparing current stock with safety stock and reorder points provides visibility into potential replenishment requirements.

3. **Product Quality:** Defective units can be analyzed at the product level to identify products contributing disproportionately to quality issues.

4. **Logistics Efficiency:** Carrier-level delay analysis helps highlight logistics providers that may need performance reviews.

5. **Revenue Concentration:** Revenue and profit comparisons across sales channels and platforms help identify major contributors to business performance.

6. **Product Profitability:** Product-level profit and discount analysis provides insights into product performance and pricing patterns.
